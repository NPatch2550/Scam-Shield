Scam Shield is an AI-powered anti-scam defense concept for iOS and Android. Its purpose is simple: help people recognize danger before sharing a secret or sending money. Instead of relying only on a blocked number or a known malicious link, we combine verified threat records with conversational context. Our hackathon scope starts with verification and user-submitted text. Real-time call and screen features are a staged roadmap, not a claim of unrestricted access to a phone.

The common thread is not a particular phone number. It is a person being persuaded to trust an impostor, disclose sensitive information, or move money under pressure. Voice scams can imitate banks or public agencies. Chat scams can appear as investment offers, romance, or paid tasks. Traditional reputation checks remain useful, but they cannot explain every new conversation. Scam Shield adds context to those checks, while acknowledging uncertainty rather than treating every unfamiliar contact as a criminal.

We capture only a supported signal or content the user has authorized. Local extraction identifies text and relevant identifiers.
